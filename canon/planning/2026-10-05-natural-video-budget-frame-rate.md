## Known policy versus proposed interpolation

The accepted policy is encoded rung R using budget B(R−2), with playback selection a separate one-rung-above-fit decision. The documented experimental ladder in `container/video-benchmark-recipes.json` and `canon/planning/2026-10-04-sovee-matched-budget-qualification.md` is240,360,480,720,1080. The measured selected720-line canvas borrows the360-class nominal450000bit/s video budget; its actual two-pass control was439024bit/s. There is no documented240-class bitrate and no540rung. Do not claim a complete historical bitrate ladder or infer a universal production default.

For this bounded experiment, explicitly propose a pixel-area interpolation anchored to that known pair. Define experimental B(240)=B(360)×(240/360)²=200000bit/s. A480-line canvas can then borrow this proposed240-class budget under the existing ladder. For540, keep the existing ladder intact and define an **off-ladder interpolated canvas**, with200000×(540/480)²=253125bit/s. Equivalently both follow450000×(height/720)². The540case is an explicit experimental extension, not a claim that inserting540 leaves all R−2 relationships unchanged. No source evidence establishes these rates as perceptually optimal; actual results may reject them.

Use these nominal numbers directly as initial two-pass target rates; do not automatically copy the a13-specific439024/450000 correction ratio. That ratio addressed a measured prior output, not a universal encoder calibration. Report actual achieved packet and total rates. Keep accepted selected-C720contract and output unchanged.
